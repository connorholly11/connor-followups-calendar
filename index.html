PERSONAL DESKTOP INTEGRATION

Connor Follow-ups

A local workspace for turning follow-ups into focused work. Review a task, estimate the time, choose an available slot, and approve the block before it is added to Google Calendar.

Calendar access

The calendar connection checks availability in selected calendars and creates private blocks in a calendar the connected user owns. Each booking requires approval of its time and details. Saved events are read back to verify the result. Blocks have no invited guests.

Local control

The desktop app runs on its owner's Mac. Google handles sign-in, and the owner can revoke the connection in Google Account permissions. This page describes the calendar integration; it does not collect calendar information or offer a sign-in form.
