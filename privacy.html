CONNOR FOLLOW-UPS · GOOGLE CALENDAR CONNECTION

Calendar privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026. This policy covers the Google Calendar connection in the personal Connor Follow-ups desktop app.

Information used

The connection uses the signed-in Google email address, the list of subscribed calendars, busy-time intervals, and the details of time blocks the owner approves. It stores a renewable Google authorization locally so the owner does not need to sign in for every request.

Purpose and permissions

Calendar information is used to suggest available time, check for conflicts, create the exact approved block, and verify that it was saved. Google’s owned-event permission also permits changing or deleting owned events; this integration implements creation and verification and does not implement editing or deleting existing calendar events. It does not change calendar sharing permissions or invite guests.

Storage and sharing

Authorization credentials and booking records are stored locally on the owner's Mac under restricted file permissions. Approved event details are sent to Google Calendar. This calendar connector does not send calendar data to AI models, use it to train models, sell it, or use it for advertising. Other optional source and agent features of the desktop workspace are separate from this calendar connection.

Owner control and retention

The owner can disconnect the app through Google Account third-party permissions. Local authorization and booking records remain until the owner removes them from the app's local data folder. Removing local records does not delete events already saved in Google Calendar; those can be managed directly in Google Calendar.

Google API user data

The integration's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Use the developer contact shown on the app's Google consent screen. This integration is operated by its owner for personal desktop use.
